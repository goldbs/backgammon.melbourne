Founding meeting · 1 December 2026

Membership administration process

Proposed process for joining or upgrading: committee approval, then payment, then membership commencement.

Draft for the founding meeting. The association is preparing to incorporate. These documents record proposals for consideration; they do not record decisions as passed or appointments as made.

Download text copy

Responsibilities

Steven · secretary
Check applications, arrange the committee decision, send written outcomes, maintain consent/member records and notify commencement.
Daniel · treasurer
Confirm legitimate account details, reconcile fees and actual receipts, and arrange receipts.
Committee
Decide formal applications by recorded resolution under model rules 10–11 and oversee the adopted rules.

1. Join the community or apply for association membership

Community participation is free and separate from formal membership. Association applicants also receive community participation. Existing community participants can use the same email; their community record and match history stay in place.

Before incorporation, adopted rules and formal opening, an association request is an expression of interest only. No deposit is requested, and no voting rights arise. Fresh agreement to the actual adopted rules is required for the later formal application.

2. Submit a complete application

Provide the name, email, phone, notice address and age category, support the purposes, read and agree to the identified rules, and confirm signature intent. Electronic signatures may be used under the supplied model rules. The secretary checks completeness and resolves discrepancies.

3. Obtain the committee decision

The committee accepts or rejects the formal application by resolution at a properly convened meeting, including permitted online meetings. The decision and actual date are recorded in the minutes. The secretary notifies the applicant in writing as soon as practicable. Any money accompanying a rejected application must be returned.

4. Request and confirm payment after approval

Accepted applicants receive the confirmed amount, account details and payment reference. Do not deposit money with an initial application. Bank details remain to be confirmed; the secretary will provide them once available.

The treasurer confirms funds actually received, matches the reference and reconciles any adjustment if the commencement month differs from an earlier estimate. Selecting the paid option or providing a payment screenshot is not itself confirmation of payment.

5. Record commencement and notify the member

Once committee approval and the applicable payment conditions are both met, the secretary records the actual commencement date and associate status in the formal register and notifies the member. A previous community signup or expression-of-interest date is not the formal commencement date.

6. Apply the approved joining-year fee

The proposed annual subscription is $100, renewing on 1 January. The proposed fixed joining-year schedule includes the joining month and is rounded to the nearest $5, under model rule 12(3)(c).

7. Confirm rights and maintain records

Under model rule 13, a non-associate can vote only after more than 10 business days of formal membership and while rights are not suspended. Under-15s are non-voting associates; committee eligibility requires age 18 or over and voting eligibility.

Unpaid annual subscriptions suspend membership rights from the due date until paid. Maintain the formal register separately from community participation records and apply the adopted rules for access, privacy and retention. Private contact, application, consent and payment records are not published in website feeds.

8. Renew and reconcile

Prepare renewal notices from the formal register, using the fee and due date determined under the adopted process. Reconcile payments and notify members of their recorded position. Application decisions, payment reconciliation and renewal communications are handled by the responsible officers.
