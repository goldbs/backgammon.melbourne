1. Open the meeting

Record actual attendance, apologies, remote participation and relevant interests. Confirm the meeting chair and minute taker.

This is the founding/incorporation meeting, not the first post-incorporation AGM. Confirm the applicable CAV formation requirements before issuing the formal notice; proposed post-incorporation rules are not assumed to be in force already.



2. Resolve to seek incorporation and confirm the name

Proposed resolution: That the founders proceed with an application to incorporate in Victoria under the Associations Incorporation Reform Act 2012, under the proposed name Melbourne Backgammon Association Inc., subject to name availability and registration.



3. Confirm the purposes

• Grow the Game: Promote the play, study, and enjoyment of backgammon across Melbourne.

• Welcome New Players: Teach newcomers and provide a comfortable place to play and learn.

• Elevate Play: Deepen strategic understanding through match transcription, analysis, and PR evaluation.

• Compete: Host regular club matches, tournaments, and chouettes.

Record approval of the final purpose text for the incorporation documents.



4. Adopt the CAV model rules

Proposed resolution: Adopt the supplied CAV model rules, completed with the final name, agreed purposes and a financial year ending 31 December.

Identify the exact document/version adopted. Model rule 10 requires a committee decision on each formal application and written notice; rule 11 requires acceptance to be minuted and commencement once approval and the applicable payment conditions are met. Community participation remains separate and automatic.

• Free community participation is separate from association membership and does not confer an association vote.

• Association membership includes community participation. Existing community participants can apply using the same email without a duplicate player record.

• Annual association subscription: $100; renewal on 1 January, subject to the adopted fee-setting process.

• Financial and membership year: 1 January–31 December.
